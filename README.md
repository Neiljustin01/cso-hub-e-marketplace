# CSO Hub & E-Marketplace

A public web portal for the Municipality of Inopacan, Leyte, Philippines, where residents can discover local Civil Society Organizations (CSOs) and reserve the products they offer, such as farm produce and food products.

**Live site:** https://csoinopacan.avenra.tech

![CSO Hub & E-Marketplace homepage](https://njperasportfolio.avenra.tech/images/systems/cso-hub.jpg)

## What it does

- Directory of accredited local CSOs with profiles, locations and products
- Product listings with prices, units and available stock
- Product reservations and inquiries, with tracking for buyers
- Separate sign-ins for buyers and CSOs, plus CSO self-registration
- Search across organizations, products, officers and members
- Distance from each seller using the buyer's location (optional)
- Available in English, Winaray, Cebuano and Filipino
